Aditya S. Gopalan is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Edwardson School of Industrial Engineering at Purdue University. He is broadly interested in applied probability, with a focus on large-scale spatial coordination systems. He uses tools from random graphs, stochastic geometry, interacting particle systems, and queueing theory.

Aditya received his bachelor's degree from MIT and his Ph.D. from University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. He is a recipient of UIUC Grainger College of Engineering's Mavis Future Faculty Fellowship and the UIUC ISE William A. Chittenden II Outstanding Graduate Student Award.
