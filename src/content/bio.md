Aditya S. Gopalan is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Edwardson School of Industrial Engineering at Purdue University. He works on establishing the fundamental capacity limits and performance bounds of spatio-temporal stochastic networks. Specifically, motivated by operational aspects of Low Earth Orbit mega-constellations and fleet-level autonomous driving, he is developing methods to couple the _temporal_ dynamics of queueing with the _spatial_ models of stochastic geometry and interacting particle systems.

Aditya received his bachelor's degree from MIT and his Ph.D. from University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. He is a recipient of UIUC Grainger College of Engineering's Mavis Future Faculty Fellowship and the UIUC ISE William A. Chittenden II Outstanding Graduate Student Award.
